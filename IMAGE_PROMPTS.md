# Image2 Prompt Pack

Use image2/image generation for bitmap travel assets. Save outputs to `public/assets`.

## General Style

Use realistic premium travel photography, natural light, polished editorial composition, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Images should work behind dark overlays and inside product cards. Avoid generic stock-photo emptiness.

Recommended dimensions:

- Hero: 2400x1400 or wider
- Cards: 1200x900
